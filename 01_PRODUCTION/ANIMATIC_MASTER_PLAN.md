## Pass 1 — editorial skeleton
For every montage shot:
1. assign provisional duration;
2. establish IN_STATE and OUT_STATE;
3. identify whether the next shot needs a matched OUT→IN frame;
4. mark screen direction;
5. place dialogue/audio;
6. use reference stills/placeholders only.

No final-generation credit is spent in Pass 1.

## Pass 2 — hero-frame lock
Create/approve only frames that control downstream continuity:
- character neutral identity frames;
- state-change frames;
- station/Saint Juniper geography masters;
- action-axis masters;
- VIAL/BOTTLE inserts;
- U*TTU silk anchor frames;
- JD first-reveal master;
- final open-door master.

## Pass 3 — motion proof
Animate only continuity-critical transitions first. A failed raccord is corrected before adjacent shots are generated.

## Shot-chain rule
Each continuity-dependent shot records:
`IN_STATE -> ACTION -> OUT_STATE -> NEXT_IN`

Accepted OUT frames become the preferred anchor for the next continuous shot. Do not regenerate a character from text when a validated preceding frame can carry identity and spatial continuity.

## Dialogue rule
Exact canonical dialogue is editorially timed before lip-sync generation. Lip-sync is generated only after duration and cut points are locked.

## Screen/text rule
No critical readable text is trusted to the video model. MITER IDs/logos, timestamps, terminal content, maps, title cards, CONTAINMENT STATUS and NO SIGNAL are composited.

## Hard-fail QC
Reject, do not patch into canon:
- wrong E*NKI heterochromia;
- iconic E*NKI outfit in pilot;
- N*ABU techwear in pilot;
- N*ABU transforming at pipe/in E*NKI's presence;
- U*TTU wrong limb count or magical silk;
- G*BOY fox/clean explanatory reveal;
- VIAL/BOTTLE confusion;
- VIAL missing after 141 where continuity requires it;
- JD weapon teleporting back after 159;
- characters leaving for Bloc hideout at 157;
- obsolete Shot179 dialogue/map behavior;
- final door closed;
- PC09 occupant revealed.
