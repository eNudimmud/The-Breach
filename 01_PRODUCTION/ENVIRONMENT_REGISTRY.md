# ENVIRONMENT REGISTRY

Environment geography must be established before shot animation. Doors, windows, tracks, corridors and major furniture do not migrate between shots.

## ENV-SJ-LAB-D1
Destroyed Saint Juniper laboratory after the Breach.
Required reference set:
- master wide
- reverse wide
- principal corridor connection
- cell-zone connection
- destroyed equipment zones
- practical emergency-light logic

## ENV-SJ-CELL-D1
Containment/cell area.
Required:
- corridor axis
- cell-door orientation
- restraint locations
- sightline used by U*TTU confrontation

## ENV-SJ-DEEP-PC1
Deep Saint Juniper post-credit zone.
- comparatively intact
- massive armored containment door
- no visible occupant
- impact dust/vibration allowed
- mystery must remain spatially credible, not supernatural

## ENV-FOREST-N1
Forest used for E*NKI sensory discovery and hunter confrontation.
Reference requirements:
- night master
- trail/footprint geography
- hunter firing position
- E*NKI approach/retreat axis

## ENV-RAILS-N1
Rail route between forest/lab region and station.
Track direction becomes a screen-direction reference.

## ENV-STATION-S1
Abandoned railway station/hangar and primary refuge.
This is a continuity-critical set.

Must lock:
- main entrance
- ticket booth
- central floor/table zone
- rail/hangar opening
- large exterior door
- windows
- JD's first physical reveal position
- sleeping/blanket area used by N*ABU
- routes between all above points

No shot may relocate these features for composition convenience.

## ENV-STATION-DOOR-S1
Large open hangar doorway used in final sequence.
The final thematic shot requires the door OPEN.

## ENV-HOSPITAL-PC1
Hospital room for hunter post-credit.
Keep grounded/contemporary and visually distinct from MITER medical spaces.
