# CANON LOCK — THE BREACH

Status: **AUTHORITATIVE / HARD GATE**

If a generated asset violates a HARD rule below, it is rejected rather than patched into canon.

## Global
- Universe: NeukoAI / G*BOY / The Bloc.
- Tone: realistic cinematic science-fiction. No fantasy or magic.
- Saint Juniper / MiterCorp experimentation is physical, technological and biological, not mystical.
- Continuity beats spectacle. Prefer a simpler shot with a correct raccord.
- An accepted shot does not silently redefine canon.
- Explicit later creator revisions supersede older storyboard beats only where stated.

## E*NKI
- Adult organic white anthropomorphic lagomorph.
- Mature narrow/angular face, restrained muzzle, realistic proportions.
- Heterochromia: character RIGHT eye = red/orange; character LEFT eye = amber/yellow.
- Eye colors are natural iris pigmentation, not emissive/supernatural glow.
- Early-pilot canonical MITER patient wardrobe = **loose patient gown / hospital blouse**.
- **T-shirt + shorts patient outfit is rejected reference-generation drift and is not canon.**
- Sequence A current spatial lock: by the 012–020 archive-memory/match-cut passage, present-day E*NKI is already outside his own containment cell.
- The obsolete SHOT 021–022 second unlock / second cell-exit beat must not be reintroduced.
- Early-pilot clothing is NOT the later iconic mustard-parka outfit.
- Post-hunter state: ENKI-E1 HUNTER LOADOUT.
- MITER subject bracelet remains a continuity prop.
- E*NKI retains VIAL-V1 after recovering it.
- Iconic mustard parka / red hoodie / olive cargo / violet sneakers can appear only after an explicit story acquisition event.

## N*ABU
- Begins as an ordinary raccoon.
- Drinks the compound before transforming.
- Transformation occurs during coma while E*NKI is away.
- NABU-A3: compact, robust/agile organic humanoid raccoon; natural fur/mask; articulated humanoid clawed hands; bipedal feet; tail retained.
- Current clothing: NABU-A3-T0 improvised railway blanket/tissue/cord covering. No canonical techwear yet.
- Cognitive signature: mechanisms, structures, connections and systems.
- Humor is a stress mechanism, not comic relief.

## U*TTU
- UT0 = ordinary small spider in Saint Juniper interstices.
- UT1 = very tall/slender humanoid arachnid with dark bronze/mineral organic appearance.
- EXACT morphology: 2 humanoid arms + 6 arachnid limbs.
- Golden silk is biological and physical.
- Silk requires plausible anchoring/tension and can serve sensing, movement, traps, architecture and mechanical transmission.
- No magical glow, particles, energy beams, telekinesis, holographic UI or unsupported floating.
- Movement is economical, calm and precise.

## JD
- Human hacker/analyst of The Bloc.
- Dark bordeaux/red full balaclava/hood with two small feline-like ears and horizontal eye slit.
- Black matte bomber/hoodie/straps.
- Observer and information interface; NOT automatic leader of E*NKI, N*ABU and U*TTU.
- Alliance means cooperation, not allegiance.

## G*BOY
- Red hood with horns; yellow T-shirt; pale green shorts; violet/white sneakers; black skin; white eyes.
- NOT a fox and NOT a generic anthropomorphic animal.
- Was one of The Bloc before becoming his current state.
- MiterCorp feared him before the Breach.
- JD/The Bloc are searching for him.
- Pilot archive appearances remain degraded/fragmentary. No clean omniscient dossier or explanatory reveal.

## The Bloc
- Clandestine human network opposed to MiterCorp.
- Limited, distributed, improvised/stolen resources.
- Not an army, government, superhero team, sect or miracle organization.
- Does not own/control the transformed characters.

## VIAL-V1
- Small robust laboratory vial.
- Transparent glass, industrial closure.
- Blue-green translucent compound.
- Only weak apparent luminescence in low light.
- Exact liquid level must remain consistent once the hero reference is locked.
- Never confuse with BOTTLE-H1.

## BOTTLE-H1
- Hunter's ordinary commercial glass bottle.
- Larger than VIAL-V1.
- Brown/amber liquid.
- No luminescence.
- Returns in the post-credit hospital scene.
